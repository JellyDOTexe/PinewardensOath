# Changelog

## 1.0.3

- Stillbough (bow) now sits in your hand. The hand was placed between the grip and the string; it now holds the wrapped grip.

## 1.0.2

- New model for Stillbough (bow). The amber ornament moved from the middle of the grip to above it, so your hand no longer covers it or clips through it.

## 1.0.1

- New icon. No gameplay changes.

## 1.0.0

- First release: ten Black Forest weapons (mace, sword, axe, war pike, sledge, atgeir, shield, dagger, spear, bow).
